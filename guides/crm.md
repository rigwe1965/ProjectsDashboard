## What it is
A customer relationship manager for small sales and support teams: contacts, companies, deals and tasks in one place.

## What it does
- Contacts and companies: searchable, filterable records, with an activity timeline for each.
- Deal pipeline: a drag-and-drop board. A reason is recorded when a deal is lost.
- Tasks and activities: open, overdue and completed views, plus a log of calls, emails, meetings and notes.
- Live dashboard: pipeline value, recent wins, win rate and what needs attention today.
- Roles and security: admin, sales and support roles, two-step verification and a full audit log.
- Open API: a documented REST API for connecting other tools.
- Light and dark themes.
